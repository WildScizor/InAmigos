About InAmigos

Built around people, not just programs.

Founded on September 23, 2020, InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 non-profit based in Chhattisgarh and working across India.

Its work brings together volunteers, professionals and partners around practical community needs, from meals and education to animal welfare, women's empowerment, environmental conservation and employability.

Why InAmigos

A foundation built to keep people involved.

InAmigos Foundation was founded in 2020 by Govind Shukla and operates from Chhattisgarh, with initiatives reaching communities across India.

Its work combines direct community programs with volunteering, awareness, skill development and partnerships.

The organization states that it is registered under Section 8 and holds 80G and 12A certifications, alongside CSR-1 and NITI Aayog registration.
